# BacaBuddy: A Document-Based Reading Assistant for Early Malay Literacy 

 BacaBuddy: A Document-Based Reading Assistant for Early Malay Literacy 

## Problem statement
Primary school in Malaysia aged 7 to 9 who a learning to read in Bahasa Melayu, along with their parents and teachers, struggle to get one to one guided reading practice. In large classroom and busy family, children rarely receive immediate, word pronunciation correction on their actual school textbook, while the existing reading apps only work with fixed English dataset, require internet, and upload children’s voice to external cloud servers. Furthermore, no open Malay children’s oral reading dataset currently exists to train and evaluate automated pronunciation scoring models for young Malaysian learners. Otherwise, personalised feedback using their everyday classroom reading materials, struggling early readers fall behind in basic Malay reading fluency and accuracy, which negatively impacts their confidence and performance across all primary school subjects.
