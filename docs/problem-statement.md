# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Young primary school children in Malaysia (aged 7 to 9) who are learning to read aloud in Bahasa Melayu, along with their parents and primary school teachers.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Children get very little one to one guided reading practice in large classrooms or busy homes. Existing reading apps only use their own fixed stories, so teachers and parents cannot use their own school textbooks, storybooks, or worksheets, and these apps are usually English-only, paid, require internet access, and send children's voice recordings to external cloud servers. Furthermore, standard speech models struggle to recognise young children's voices, and no open Malay children's reading speech dataset currently exists.

## Why does it matter?
Without immediate, word by word pronunciation feedback on their actual classroom reading materials, struggling young readers fall behind in basic Bahasa Melayu reading fluency and accuracy, which lowers their confidence and hurts their learning across all school subjects. In addition, paid cloud based apps create a cost barrier for low income schools and families while exposing underage children's voice data to privacy risks.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Young primary school children in Malaysia aged 7 to 9 who are learning to read in Bahasa Melayu, along with their parents and teachers, struggle to get enough one to one guided oral reading practice. In large classrooms and busy homes, children rarely receive immediate pronunciation correction on their actual school textbooks or worksheets, while existing reading apps only support fixed English stories, require paid internet subscriptions, and upload children's voices to external cloud servers. Furthermore, standard speech recognition models perform poorly on young children's voices, and no open Malay children's reading dataset currently exists to support accurate Malay pronunciation scoring. As a result, struggling early readers fall behind in basic Bahasa Melayu reading fluency and accuracy without affordable, privacy safe support, which negatively impacts their confidence and overall school performance.
